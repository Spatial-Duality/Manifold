Apple Ideas approach. One colour, no gradient, no texture. The mark does all the work.
Maximum confidence. Reads as a brand decision, not a design accident.

2 — Linear gradient
Top-light, bottom-deep. Adds quiet dimensionality without changing the colour story.
Reads as "considered" without losing the saffron commit. Reduces the flat-icon problem at small sizes.
Slightly conventional. Every gradient app on the App Store does this — uniqueness is borrowed.
At dock size (64pt) Atmosphere reads as "warmth"
3 — Radial cloud
Two soft radial centres (light upper-left, deeper lower-right) blended into the saffron base. Static.
Atmospheric without animation. Each render looks like a moment of light, not a flat colour. OpenAI-recommended.
Slightly less iconic than solid — the icon's silhouette has to do more work.

4 — Animated cloud (web only)
The radial centres slowly drift across an 18-second cycle. For the marketing site hero, not the app icon.
Looks alive without flashing. OpenAI's "atmosphere" recommendation in motion. Hero-grade.
Don't put this on the dock icon — it can't animate. Web-only.
At dock size (64pt) Paper-grain feel — tactile
5 — Grain texture
Solid saffron with a fine SVG-noise overlay (≈18% multiply). Imperceptible at first glance, premium on second.
Defeats the "flat icon" criticism without resorting to gradient. Pixelmator/Affinity-grade tactility.
Subtle effect — at very small scales the grain disappears. Not a hero gesture.
Recommended for the icon Radial cloud + 10% grain. Tactile + atmospheric.
6 — Hybrid (radial + grain) — recommended
Radial cloud (treatment 3) with a faint grain layer (treatment 5 at 10%). Combines atmosphere with tactility.
Looks crafted at every scale: dock icon, splash, screenshot. Holds up against Pixelmator/Things in the same dock. The "static cloud + grain" pattern is exactly what Apple Ideas does, just in saffron instead of mustard.
More complex to ship in Icon Composer than a flat fill — requires layering. Worth it.

Recommendation: treatment 6 (hybrid)

The Apple Ideas reference is not really "solid yellow" once you look closely at it — there's a gentle radial warmth from the lightbulb's centre and a fine paper-like grain across the canvas. The flat-yellow appearance is a render artefact at thumbnail scale.

Treatment 6 reproduces what makes Ideas read as crafted: one colour commit (saffron #F5B400), plus subtle atmosphere (radial cloud), plus subtle tactility (10% grain). The mark sits on top in dark ink. Total file size when shipped as PNG renditions: still under 200KB.

For the icon (Icon Composer): use treatment 6 as the static base. Skip animation — the icon doesn't need to move.

For the splash (TitleSequence): use treatment 6 as the settle background, with the mark animating in via SF Symbols 7 Draw On. The settle = ~1.5s of the cloud + mark holding still before the wordmark fades in.

For the marketing site hero: use treatment 4 (animated cloud) — slow drift adds life to the page hero where the user has time to notice.

For everything else in the app: the saffron (#F5B400) is a brand accent only — sparse, on hover backgrounds, on the single CTA per first-run panel, on the easter-egg spin. Status colours stay semantic.
